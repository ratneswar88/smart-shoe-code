Smart Shoe Carrier PCB — Rev A

POWERBOOST 500C · ESP32-S3 ZERO · GY-86 · PLUG-AND-PLAY CARRIER

75mm × 35mm 1.6mm FR4 2-LAYER ALL CONNECTIONS PRE-ROUTED
📐

Why not 40×20mm? — PowerBoost 500C is 35.6 × 22.9mm by itself. ESP32-S3 Zero is 25.4 × 25.4mm. GY-86 is 20 × 15mm. They cannot physically share a 40×20mm board placed side by side. The minimum viable carrier is 75mm × 35mm with modules arranged in a single row. Everything is pre-routed — you simply drop each module into its socket and the board handles all connections.
