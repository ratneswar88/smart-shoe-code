# 30-Day Rock Lead Guitar Masterplan
## From Scales to Slash: Your Complete Journey

**Styles Covered:** David Gilmour (Pink Floyd), Slash (Guns N' Roses), Classic Rock, Modern Rock, Blues, Acoustic & Electric

**Your Current Level:** Know scales and basics, ready to shape melodies

- 20 min: Technical exercises
- 30 min: Style study & application

## WEEK 1: FOUNDATIONS - The Language of Lead Guitar

### **DAY 1: The Pentatonic Connection (Electric)**
**Focus:** A minor pentatonic - Your foundation for everything

**Morning Routine (20 min):**
```
Position 1 (5th fret):
e|--5--8-----------------------
B|--5--8-----------------------
G|--5--7-----------------------
D|--5--7-----------------------
A|--5--7-----------------------
E|--5--8-----------------------
```

1. Play straight up/down (2 min)
2. Practice string skipping: play only on E-G-A strings, then B-D strings (3 min)
3. Bending exercise: 7th fret G string, bend to pitch of 9th fret (5 min)
4. Create three 2-bar phrases, record them (10 min)

**Style Study (30 min):**
- Learn: "Another Brick in the Wall Pt 2" solo (Gilmour) - first 8 bars
- Notice: Long sustained notes, slow bends, vibrato on every note
- Key lesson: Gilmour plays FEWER notes with MORE feeling

**Jam Session (10 min):**
- Backing track: A minor blues rock (YouTube)
- Goal: Play only 4-note phrases, rest for 4 beats between phrases

**Listening Assignment:**
- "Comfortably Numb" second solo
- Count how many notes Gilmour plays in the first 8 bars (hint: not many!)

**Gear Setup:**
- Electric guitar, bridge pickup
- Light overdrive/distortion
- Reverb

### **DAY 2: Adding the Blue Note (Electric)**
**Focus:** The b5 (blue note) - Makes rock sound "bluesy"

**Technical (20 min):**

Minor Pentatonic + Blue Note:
```
e|--5--8-----------------------
B|--5--8-----------------------
G|--5--6--7--------------------  ← The 6 is your blue note
D|--5--7-----------------------
A|--5--7-----------------------
E|--5--8-----------------------
```

Exercise: "Tension & Release"
```
G|--5--6--7--6--5-------------  (the 6 creates tension)
```

Practice this 20 times with different rhythms

**Style Study (30 min):**
- Learn: "Sweet Child O' Mine" intro melody (Slash)
- Notice: Uses major pentatonic, chromatic passing tones
- Key lesson: Slash mixes major and minor sounds

**Jam Session (15 min):**

**Daily Challenge:**
Create one 4-bar phrase using the blue note at least twice

### **DAY 3: Vibrato - Your Voice (Electric)**
**Focus:** Developing killer vibrato like Gilmour

**Technical (20 min):**

Three types of vibrato:
1. **Narrow vibrato** (small, fast wobbles)
2. **Wide vibrato** (Gilmour style - slow, wide)
3. **Delayed vibrato** (hit note, wait, then vibrato)

Exercise:
```
B|--8---------------------------  
Hold this note for 8 counts with wide, slow vibrato
```

Practice each string, each fret from 5-12

**Gilmour Vibrato Study (30 min):**

Learn "Shine On You Crazy Diamond" opening solo (first 16 bars):
```
B|--10b12~~~~~~~--12~~~~~~~--10~~~~~~~
```
The "~" means vibrato - this is 90% of the solo!

**Key Gilmour Techniques:**
- Pre-bend then pick (sounds vocal-like)
- Vibrato on EVERY sustained note
- Wide, slow vibrato (like singing)

**Jam Session (15 min):**
- Play long, sustained notes with vibrato
- Focus on making guitar "sing"

**Assignment:**
Record yourself playing one sustained note with vibrato. Does it sound smooth?

### **DAY 6: Bending Bootcamp (Electric)**
**Focus:** Mastering all bend types

**Technical Workout (25 min):**

**Type 1: Whole-step bends**
```
G|--7b9---  (must sound exactly like 9th fret)
```

**Type 2: Half-step bends (blues)**
```
G|--7b8---
```

**Type 3: Pre-bend & release (Gilmour signature)**
```
G|--(9)r7---  (bend before picking, then release)
```

**Type 4: Bend & vibrato combo**
```
G|--7b9~~~~~~~
```

**Type 5: Unison bends (advanced)**
```
B|--8---------
G|--7b9-------  (bend G string to match B string pitch)
```

Practice each 10 times per string

**Song Application (30 min):**
Learn: "Comfortably Numb" second solo (bars 1-16)
- 80% bends
- Note Gilmour's slow, controlled bends

**Challenge:**
Record yourself bending 7th fret to 9th on G string
Play it back - is it in tune?

Blues box 1 (same as minor pentatonic but contextualized):

```
e|--5--8-----------------------
B|--5--8-----------------------

A|--5--7-----------------------
E|--5--8-----------------------
```

### **DAY 12: The Texas Blues Shuffle (Electric)**
**Focus:** SRV, ZZ Top style

**Technical (25 min):**

Texas shuffle rhythm (behind the beat feel):
```
        1  &  2  &  3  &  4  &
A7: |  x  x  x  x  x  x  x  x |
(swing the 8th notes - triplet feel)
```

Lead lines with shuffle feel:
```
e|----------------------------------
B|--5--8--5--8--5------------------
G|----------------7--5--7----------
(play with triplet subdivision)
```

**SRV Study (30 min):**

Learn: "Pride and Joy" intro lick

```
e|----------------------------------
B|----------------------------------
G|--6b7--6--4-----4----------------
D|-------------6------6--4---------
A|---------------------------7--5--
E|----------------------------------
```

Key SRV techniques:
- Aggressive attack
- Fast vibrato

**Application (15 min):**
Texas shuffle backing track

### **DAY 13: Slide Guitar Basics (Acoustic/Electric)**
**Focus:** Opening new sonic territory

**Technical (25 min):**

**Slide Setup:**
- Glass or brass slide on pinky or ring finger
- Open tuning helps (Open G: D-G-D-G-B-D)
- Or stay in standard tuning

**Slide Technique:**
- Slide sits DIRECTLY over fret wire (not behind like fretting)
- Don't press down
- Mute strings behind slide with other fingers

Basic exercise:
```
e|---/5---/7---/10---/12-------  (/ = slide up)
```

**Study (30 min):**

Learn: "In My Time of Dying" (Led Zeppelin)

**Jamming (15 min):**
Open G tuning, just explore sounds with slide

**Workout (30 min):**

## APPENDIX B: Gear Recommendations

### **Electric Setup**

**Essential:**
- Electric guitar (single coils for Gilmour, humbuckers for Slash)
- Amplifier (modeling amp like Fender Mustang works great)
- Cable
- Picks (medium-heavy, 0.88-1.0mm)

**Recommended Effects:**
- Overdrive/Distortion pedal
- Delay pedal (essential for Gilmour)
- Reverb
- Chorus (for clean tones)

**Gilmour-specific:**
- Compression (subtle)
- Long delay times
- Lots of reverb

**Slash-specific:**
- Marshall-style overdrive
- Short delay
- Wah pedal (for funk sections)

### **Acoustic Setup**

**Essential:**
- Acoustic guitar (dreadnought or OM)
- Extra light strings for bends (.011-.050)
- Fingerpicks (optional)
- Capo

**Recording:**
- USB audio interface
- DAW software (free: Audacity, GarageBand)
- Backing track apps (YouTube, iReal Pro)
